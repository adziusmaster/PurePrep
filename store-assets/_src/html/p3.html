Mushroom Risotto
Ingredients
  • 500 g mushrooms, sliced 3 mm thick
  • 1¼ cups arborio rice
  • 5 cups warm chicken stock
  • ½ cup parmesan, finely grated
Method
  1. 1Sauté mushrooms in butter until golden, then set aside.
  2. 2Soften shallots and garlic; add rice and toast one minute.
  3. 3Add stock a ladle at a time until creamy and just cooked.
