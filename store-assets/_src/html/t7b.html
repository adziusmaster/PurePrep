Mushroom Risotto
Ingredients
  • 500 g mushrooms, sliced 3 mm thick
  • 250 g mushrooms, quartered
  • 1¼ cups arborio rice
  • 5 cups warm chicken stock
  • ½ cup dry white wine
  • ½ cup parmesan, finely grated
  • 3 tbsp butter · 2 tbsp olive oil
Method
  1. 1Sauté mushrooms in butter until golden; set aside.
  2. 2Soften shallots and garlic, then toast the rice.
  3. 3Deglaze with wine and let it evaporate.
  4. 4Add stock a ladle at a time until creamy and just cooked.
  5. 5Stir through parmesan and the mushrooms; serve.
