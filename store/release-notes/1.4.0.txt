<en-GB>
What's new in 1.4.0
• Share or copy a recipe link — PurePrep offers to import it.
• Search recipes on the web in-app, then "Add to PurePrep".
• Smarter imports: named timers, servings, times, a photo.
• Scale by people: Serves − 4 +.
• Focus Mode always fits the step: "You'll need", Read/Stop, timers bar.
• New editor: one row per ingredient/step, drag to reorder, add photos.
• Notes, favourites, Want to cook / Cooked, sorting.
• Dialogs got a fresh look.
</en-GB>
